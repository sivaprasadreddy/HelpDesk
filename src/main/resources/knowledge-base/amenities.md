# Amenities

## Swimming pool
The swimming pool is open from 6 AM to 9 AM and from 4 PM to 8 PM. It is closed on Mondays for cleaning.
Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Proper swimwear is required.

## Gym
The gym in the clubhouse is open from 5 AM to 10 PM every day. Residents must wipe down equipment after use.
Personal trainers from outside must be registered at the association office.

## Clubhouse and party hall
The party hall can be booked for private events for a fee of 2,000 rupees for up to 4 hours, plus a refundable deposit of 5,000 rupees.
Bookings are made through the association office at least 7 days in advance. Music must stop by 10 PM.

## Children's play area
The play area is open from 6 AM to 8 PM. Children under 6 must be supervised by an adult.

## Guest rooms
Two guest rooms in the clubhouse can be booked for up to 3 nights at 1,500 rupees per night.
