# Community Rules

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 7 AM every day. Loud music, drilling and hammering are not allowed during quiet hours.
Renovation work inside flats is allowed only between 9 AM and 6 PM on weekdays and 10 AM to 4 PM on Saturdays. No renovation work is allowed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Pets
Residents may keep up to two pets per flat. Dogs must be on a leash in all common areas and are not allowed in the swimming pool area, the gym or the children's play area.
Pet owners must clean up after their pets. Pets must be registered with the association office along with their vaccination certificates.

## Parking
Each flat has one allotted covered parking slot. Visitor parking is near Gate 2 and is limited to 4 hours.
Vehicles must not block driveways, fire lanes or another resident's slot. Vehicles parked in the wrong place may be clamped by security.
Car washing is allowed only in the designated washing bay near Block D, between 6 AM and 10 AM.

## Waste disposal
Waste must be segregated into wet (green bin), dry (blue bin) and hazardous (red bin) waste.
Housekeeping collects waste from every floor between 7 AM and 9 AM. Large items such as old furniture must be booked for pickup through the association office.

## Visitors and deliveries
All visitors must register at the main gate. Residents can pre-approve visitors with the gate pass in the community app.
Delivery staff may go up to the flat only if the resident approves the delivery when security calls.
