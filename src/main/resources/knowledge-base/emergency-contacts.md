# Emergency Contacts

## Security desk
The main gate security desk is staffed 24 hours a day and can be reached on intercom 100 or 080-4000-1000.

## Fire
In case of fire, use the stairs, never the lift. Fire extinguishers are on every floor near the staircase. Assembly point is the open lawn near Gate 1.
Call the fire service on 101 and inform security on intercom 100.

## Medical emergencies
Call an ambulance on 108. A first aid kit and an automated external defibrillator (AED) are kept at the security desk.
The nearest hospital is City Care Hospital, 2 km from the main gate.

## Lift breakdown
If you are stuck in a lift, press the alarm button and stay calm. Security and the lift technician are alerted automatically.
Do not try to force the doors open.

## Power and water outages
For a power failure affecting a whole block, the backup generator starts within 30 seconds and supports lifts and common lights.
Scheduled water supply interruptions are announced on the notice board and in the community app at least 24 hours in advance.
