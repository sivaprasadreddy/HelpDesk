# Maintenance FAQ

## How do I report a maintenance issue?
Raise a ticket in HelpDesk with a clear title, a description of the problem and where it is (block, flat and floor), and choose the matching category, such as Plumbing or Electrical.
You can attach up to three photos of the problem. The ticket is assigned automatically to a staff member who handles that category.

## How quickly will my ticket be handled?
Critical issues, such as a burst pipe, a power failure for the whole flat or a person stuck in a lift, are attended to within 1 hour.
High priority issues are attended to within 4 hours, medium within 24 hours and low priority within 3 days.

## What do the ticket statuses mean?
Open means the ticket is waiting for work to start. In Progress means a staff member is working on it.
Resolved means the work is done; check it and add a comment if the problem is still there. Closed means the ticket is complete.

## Which repairs are paid for by the association?
The association pays for repairs to common areas, lifts, common pipelines, the main electrical supply and the building structure.
Repairs inside a flat, such as a leaking tap, a faulty switch or a broken cupboard, are charged to the resident for parts; labour by HelpDesk staff is free for the first 30 minutes.

## Annual maintenance charges
Maintenance charges are 3 rupees per square foot per month, payable quarterly by the 10th of the first month of the quarter.
A late fee of 2 percent per month applies to overdue payments.
